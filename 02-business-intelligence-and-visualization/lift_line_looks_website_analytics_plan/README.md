## Project Overview

Lift Line Looks is a fictional Vail-based retailer specializing in vintage-inspired ski and snowboard apparel. As online sales become a larger part of its business, the company needs a structured way to understand how customers discover, navigate, and interact with its website.

This project develops a website analytics plan connecting the retailer’s business objectives to measurable customer behaviors. It defines the questions the organization should answer, the KPIs needed to evaluate performance, the systems required to collect reliable data, and a reporting structure that delivers relevant information to different stakeholders.

## Business Questions

The analytics plan is structured around several decisions affecting the company’s digital performance:

- Which aspects of website design and navigation most influence conversion?
- How does average order value differ across customer groups and campaigns?
- Where does friction occur during the checkout process?
- Which marketing channels produce traffic that converts into customers?
- Which pages experience the greatest visitor disengagement?
- Which behaviors indicate repeat purchasing and long-term retention?

These questions ensure that data collection remains connected to business decisions rather than producing disconnected website statistics.

## Measurement Framework

| Metric | Business purpose |
|---|---|
| **Conversion Rate** | Evaluate how effectively website visits result in desired actions or purchases |
| **Average Order Value** | Measure transaction value and assess promotions, bundles, and cross-selling |
| **Cart Abandonment Rate** | Identify where customers encounter friction before completing a purchase |
| **Traffic Sources** | Determine which acquisition channels generate valuable website activity |
| **Bounce Rate** | Identify pages where content or user experience may not meet visitor expectations |
| **Customer Retention Rate** | Evaluate repeat purchasing and the strength of long-term customer relationships |

## Proposed Data Environment

The plan recommends connecting information from several parts of the digital customer journey:

- A web analytics platform for visitor activity and navigation behavior
- An e-commerce platform for transactions, carts, products, and payments
- A CRM system for purchase history, communication, and loyalty indicators
- Marketing systems for email, campaign, and channel performance
- A centralized data platform for integrating information across sources
- Business intelligence software for dashboards and stakeholder reporting

Bringing these sources together would allow Lift Line Looks to examine the relationship between marketing activity, website behavior, purchasing decisions, and customer retention.

## Analytical Approach

The proposed framework moves through three levels of analysis. Descriptive analytics summarize current website and sales performance, while diagnostic analysis explores why those patterns occur through segmentation, funnel analysis, and time-based comparisons. As the company’s analytics capabilities mature, predictive techniques could support seasonal forecasting, customer lifetime value analysis, personalization, and identification of customers most likely to convert.

The plan also recommends an iterative feedback process in which analytical findings inform website improvements, campaign adjustments, and retention strategies. Performance can then be measured again to determine whether those changes produced the intended result.

## Stakeholder Reporting

Reporting is tailored to how different audiences use information:

- **Executives** receive concise scorecards focused on growth, profitability, and customer experience.
- **Managers** receive campaign, channel, and conversion analysis that supports resource allocation and tactical decisions.
- **Analysts** receive detailed data and supporting visualizations for modeling, testing, and continued investigation.

This tiered structure provides each audience with appropriate detail while maintaining a centralized source of reporting information.

## Future Analytics Roadmap

The plan recommends that Lift Line Looks gradually expand beyond performance reporting by establishing data-governance standards, improving data quality, and developing predictive capabilities. Future analysis could support demand forecasting, product-trend identification, customer personalization, and more proactive business decisions.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Translating business objectives into analytical questions
- Defining KPIs and their decision-making purpose
- Planning cross-platform data collection
- Connecting website behavior to business outcomes
- Designing a stakeholder-focused reporting strategy
- Applying descriptive, diagnostic, and predictive analytics concepts
- Incorporating data governance into an analytics roadmap
