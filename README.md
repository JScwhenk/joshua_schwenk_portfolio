## About Me

I am a recent Colorado State University Global graduate with a degree in Management Information Systems and Business Analytics. This portfolio presents selected projects demonstrating how I use data, technology, and structured analysis to solve business problems.

Before completing my degree, I spent six years in talent acquisition. That experience gave me a practical understanding of business operations, stakeholder needs, process improvement, and the importance of reliable information. I am now combining that background with technical work in analytics, business intelligence, database development, data modeling, and information systems.

## About the Projects

The work in this portfolio began as part of my undergraduate studies and has been reorganized for a professional audience. Each project focuses on the business problem, technical approach, analytical process, and resulting insights rather than presenting the original assignment alone.

As projects are added, their folders will include concise documentation alongside relevant source code, data models, dashboard previews, visualizations, and downloadable files.
