## Overview

The projects in this section demonstrate how statistical analysis and predictive modeling can be used to examine data, test relationships, identify meaningful patterns, and estimate future outcomes. The work covers exploratory analysis, probability and distributions, hypothesis testing, regression, classification, clustering, model evaluation, and the interpretation of analytical results.

Together, these projects emphasize both technical execution and responsible interpretation. Each analysis considers the business or research question, the suitability of the selected method, the performance and limitations of the model, and how the findings can inform practical decisions.
