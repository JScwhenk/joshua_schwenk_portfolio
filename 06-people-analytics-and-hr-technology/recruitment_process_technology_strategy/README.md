## Project Overview

This project evaluates how ABC Company could replace its paper-based recruitment process with a more organized digital workflow. The existing process relies on physical applications, handwritten feedback, verbal communication, and informal candidate comparisons.

The proposed strategy introduces technology throughout sourcing, application, screening, and selection while considering candidate experience, implementation challenges, algorithmic bias, and the continued need for human judgment.

---

## Proposed Improvements

- Introduce an applicant tracking system to centralize candidate records and recruiting activity
- Expand sourcing through online recruiting platforms and searchable talent databases
- Replace paper applications with a consistent online application process
- Use digital screening tools to organize applications and identify baseline qualifications
- Standardize interviewer feedback through shared scorecards and evaluation criteria
- Preserve human review for consequential candidate decisions
