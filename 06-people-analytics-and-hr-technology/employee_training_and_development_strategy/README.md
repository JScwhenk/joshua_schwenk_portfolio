## Project Overview

This project brings together three presentations examining how organizations can support employees from initial onboarding through continued development and advancement. The work connects recruitment and employee fit with orientation, mentorship, management preparation, e-learning, and professional development.

Together, the presentations demonstrate how structured training can improve employee readiness, engagement, performance, and retention.

---

## Project Components

### Human Resources Training Strategy

A comprehensive workforce-development plan covering recruitment technology, person-job and person-organization fit, employee assessments, onboarding, training, and retention.

[View the Human Resources Training Strategy](./human_resources_training_strategy.pdf)

### New Employee Orientation Plan

A structured orientation agenda introducing employees to organizational culture, policies, benefits, technology, departments, workplace resources, and their first weeks of training.

[View the New Employee Orientation Plan](./new_employee_orientation_plan.pdf)

### Management Training Plan

A hybrid development program designed to prepare prospective managers through leadership education, practical skill development, workshops, online learning, and measurable training objectives.

[View the Management Training Plan](./management_training_plan.pdf)

## Skills Demonstrated

**Training Strategy:** Development of structured learning experiences for employees and managers

**Onboarding:** Planning orientation, organizational socialization, and early employee support

**Talent Management:** Connecting selection, development, engagement, and retention

**Program Evaluation:** Consideration of learning objectives, delivery methods, and organizational return on investment
