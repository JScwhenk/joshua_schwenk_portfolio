## Collection Overview

This collection examines how organizations can use data and artificial intelligence while maintaining quality, security, fairness, transparency, and accountability. The studies move from enterprise-wide data management to focused cases involving hiring automation, algorithmic bias, document quality, and poorly governed information systems.

Together, the work demonstrates that responsible technology requires more than technical capability. Organizations must establish clear ownership, reliable data standards, appropriate human oversight, and governance processes that consider how automated decisions affect employees, customers, and other stakeholders.

## Enterprise Data Management Plan

This project develops a data-management strategy for an organization with approximately 5,000 employees and information distributed across internal and external systems. The plan addresses data quality, content management, master and reference data, cybersecurity, business intelligence, and organizational accountability.

It also considers how governance standards can support responsible automation by establishing clear ownership, access controls, quality expectations, and processes for monitoring how organizational data is collected and used.

**Focus:** Enterprise Governance • Data Quality • Cybersecurity • Content Management • Accountability

## The Ethical Dilemma of AI in Hiring Decisions

This research paper examines the benefits and ethical risks of using artificial intelligence in recruitment. AI can improve efficiency, automate repetitive work, and generate insights from hiring data, but fully automated decisions can reinforce historical discrimination, obscure accountability, and overlook important human context.

The analysis recommends a human-in-the-loop approach supported by transparency, candidate notification, explainability, regular bias testing, opportunities for appeal, and continued organizational oversight.

**Focus:** AI Ethics • Algorithmic Bias • Explainability • Human Oversight • Talent Acquisition

[View the Ethical AI in Hiring Analysis](./ethical_ai_in_hiring.pdf)

## HR Automation and Data Ethics

This case study evaluates the use of automation in hiring and onboarding when the underlying records are outdated, inconsistent, or retained without clear purpose. It considers how weak document management and poor data quality can reduce the reliability of automated recommendations and introduce unfair outcomes.

The case emphasizes that automation should be evaluated through both operational results and governance measures. Efficiency gains must be considered alongside accuracy, bias risk, employee impact, and the quality of the information driving the process.

**Focus:** HR Automation • Document Quality • Data Retention • Bias Risk • Performance Measurement

[View the HR Automation and Data Ethics Case](./hr_automation_data_ethics_case.pdf)

## ERP Data Quality and Governance

This case analyzes an enterprise resource planning environment affected by non-normalized data, inconsistent standards, duplicated information, and uncontrolled table creation. These weaknesses make reporting less reliable and increase the difficulty of maintaining a consistent organizational view of information.

The analysis considers whether the organization should remediate the existing environment or pursue system replacement. It demonstrates how database structure, governance responsibility, and organizational practices must be evaluated together when addressing enterprise data-quality problems.

**Focus:** ERP Governance • Data Standards • Data Quality • Normalization • Systems Evaluation
