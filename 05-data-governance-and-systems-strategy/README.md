## Overview

The projects in this section examine how organizations can develop trustworthy data environments and align technology investments with operational needs. The work covers data quality, ownership, security, systems integration, cloud architecture, stakeholder access, implementation planning, and responsible uses of analytics and artificial intelligence.

Together, these projects demonstrate how technical solutions must be supported by clear requirements, governance standards, organizational accountability, and strategies that allow information to remain reliable as systems and business needs evolve.

---

## Projects

*Projects marked with ⭐ represent highlighted portfolio work.*

### ⭐ [Logistics Intelligence Platform](../01-data-platforms-and-engineering/logistics-intelligence-platform)

An integrated business intelligence solution that combines operational reporting with a broader data-governance and implementation strategy. In addition to its relational model and Power BI dashboards, the project addresses data quality, ownership, reporting consistency, security, stakeholder access, and the organizational processes needed to maintain a reliable analytics environment.

**Concepts:** Data Governance • Reporting Strategy • Data Quality • Stakeholder Access • Implementation Planning

### ⭐ [Enterprise BI & Cloud Data Strategy](./enterprise_bi_and_cloud_data_strategy)

An enterprise technology strategy developed to improve reporting across disconnected business systems. The proposal evaluates cloud data environments, system integration, centralized analytics, semantic modeling, and business intelligence tools while establishing a scalable approach to data access, quality, and organizational reporting.

**Concepts:** Enterprise BI • Cloud Architecture • Systems Integration • Data Strategy • Implementation Planning

### ⭐ [Direct-to-Consumer Ordering System Strategy](./direct_to_consumer_system_strategy)

A systems-analysis and design project developed to support an organization’s transition to direct-to-consumer sales. The project translates business needs into requirements, process and system models, feasibility considerations, and an implementation strategy for a customer-facing ordering environment.

**Concepts:** Systems Analysis • Requirements Development • Process Modeling • Feasibility Analysis • Implementation Strategy

### [Responsible Data & AI Governance Studies](./responsible_data_and_ai_governance)

A collection of governance and ethics studies examining how organizations can protect data quality, privacy, security, fairness, and accountability as information is collected and used. The work also considers responsible AI adoption, algorithmic bias, human oversight, and the policies needed to manage high-impact technology decisions.

**Concepts:** Data Governance • Data Ethics • Privacy & Security • Algorithmic Fairness • Responsible AI
